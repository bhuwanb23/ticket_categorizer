Description

What you're building

You'll receive a small labeled dataset of support tickets/emails (subject + body text), each already tagged with a category — Billing, Technical, HR, or General. Your job is to build a model that reads new, unseen ticket text and predicts its category automatically.

Load and clean the text data → convert it into numerical features (TF-IDF or simple keyword vectors) → train a classifier (Naive Bayes or Logistic Regression) → evaluate accuracy → predict the category for at least 5 new sample tickets you write yourself.

Treat this as a real internal tool: assume it will sit in front of a live ticket queue and needs to make a fast, defensible decision on every incoming message.
